Bubble Reef Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026 · Bubble Reef by PiWare

Bubble Reef is a puzzle game. We built it so that it does not collect, store or share any personal information about you. This policy explains what that means in practice.

What we collect

Nothing. Bubble Reef has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information, and we do not sell or share data with anyone. The game makes no internet connections while you play: everything it needs, including its artwork and fonts, is built into the app.

What stays on your device

To save your game, Bubble Reef keeps a few things in the app's own storage on your device:

  • your level progress, stars and best scores,
  • a game in progress, so you can pick up where you left off,
  • your fish tank collection, and
  • your sound settings, and which how-to-play guides you have already seen.

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

App stores

If you download Bubble Reef from Google Play or the App Store, Google or Apple may collect information about your download and device under their own privacy policies. We do not receive personal information from them.

Permissions

Bubble Reef asks for no special permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Children

Bubble Reef does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Bubble Reef changes how it handles information, for example by adding ads or online features, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
